## Home page module

Add a short section after the main introduction:

### A safer home is built one ordinary moment at a time

People often arrive here feeling overwhelmed by the whole stepfamily story at once.

But safety in a home is usually not built through one grand insight.
It is built through tone, timing, repair, and the small repeated choice to reduce fear inside the house.

That means:

- telling the truth gently
- protecting children from divided loyalty pressure
- strengthening the couple bond without making the child the problem
- choosing repair over scorekeeping

If you need a next step, start small.
Lower the emotional temperature.
Protect belonging.
Choose the next honest act of steadiness.

## Start here page module

Add a section called:

### If the house feels tense, begin here

- Do not try to solve the whole story tonight.
- Bring the temperature down before you bring explanations in.
- Protect the child from becoming the emotional container for the room.
- Speak privately with your partner before deciding publicly.
- Let repair be more important than appearance.
