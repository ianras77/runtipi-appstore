# Launch Posts

## 1. If tonight felt tense, it does not mean your family is broken

Short reassurance-driven piece.

Key points:

- one hard night is not the whole truth of a family
- the next wise step is usually smaller than people think
- lower tone, protect the child, reconnect privately, let tomorrow hold some of the weight

## 2. How partners can get aligned without making the child the problem

Framework post.

Key points:

- healthy unity is quieter than a shared case against the child
- speak privately before deciding publicly
- agree on tone as much as outcome
- become a shelter, not a tribunal

## 3. When a child seems distant, protect belonging before you pursue closeness

Trust-building post.

Key points:

- distance is often caution or grief, not rejection
- remove pressure to prove warmth
- belonging sounds like patience, not pursuit

## 4. Three sentences for the morning after conflict

Short practical post.

Suggested lines:

1. Last night was hard, and I want to bring a calmer tone today.
2. You do not have to fix everything right now for us to begin again.
3. When you are ready, I want to talk in a way that helps this house feel safer.
