## Core message

This is a room built on steadiness, dignity, and truthful care.

We do not gather around child-bashing.
We do not teach through fear.
We do not use shame as a shortcut to clarity.

We protect:

- dignity for every person in the family
- child-respecting language
- practical support that lowers reactivity
- room for grief, mixed feelings, and repair
- guidance that helps the home feel more like shelter than battleground
