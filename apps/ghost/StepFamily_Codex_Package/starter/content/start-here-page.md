## Page copy

If you are new here, begin with this:

You do not need to solve the whole family story this week.
You need the next honest step.

For most stepfamilies, that step looks like one of four things:

### 1. Lower the emotional temperature

Before you explain, correct, or chase understanding, slow the room down.
People hear better when they no longer feel threatened.

### 2. Protect belonging

Do not ask children to prove comfort, loyalty, or closeness on command.
Belonging grows best in an atmosphere with less pressure and more steadiness.

### 3. Strengthen adult alignment privately

Couple unity matters, but it should feel like shelter, not a joint prosecution.
Speak together behind the scenes so the child does not become the center of adult tension.

### 4. Choose repair over image

The family does not need perfect adults.
It needs adults who can tell the truth, soften, reset, and begin again.
