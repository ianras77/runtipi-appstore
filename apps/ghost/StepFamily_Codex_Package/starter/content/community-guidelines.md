## Page copy

This community is built for people carrying real tenderness.
That means the tone of the room matters.

We want readers and participants to feel safer after spending time here, not more inflamed, more ashamed, or more certain that somebody in the home must be the villain.

### What we protect

- dignity for every person in the family
- honest language without cruelty
- practical support that lowers reactivity
- child-respecting guidance
- room for mixed feelings, grief, hope, and repair

### What does not belong here

- contempt toward children
- dehumanizing language about former partners or co-parents
- “win at all costs” advice that makes the home feel more dangerous
- rigid certainty about people you do not know
- public pile-ons, humiliation, or superiority performances

### What good participation looks like

- speaking from lived experience instead of moral theater
- telling the truth without making yourself the hero
- offering ideas that restore calm, clarity, and accountability
- naming pain without turning it into identity
- making space for slow trust instead of demanding instant harmony

### Editorial guardrails

Before publishing, posting, or promoting a piece of writing, ask:

1. Does this help the reader become wiser or just more afraid?
2. Does this protect the child’s dignity?
3. Does this give the adults something honest and doable?
4. Would someone in a tender season feel steadier after reading it?
5. Are we offering clarity without shaming the reader?

If the answer to those questions is mostly no, the piece needs more work.

### Safety note

This community offers reflection, education, and support.
It is not a crisis line, and it is not a substitute for emergency, legal, or clinical care.
If someone is in immediate danger, use local emergency resources right away.
